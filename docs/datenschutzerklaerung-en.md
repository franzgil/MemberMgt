# Privacy Policy – Membership of AFOL.lu a.s.b.l.

## 1. Controller

The controller responsible for processing your personal data is:

**AFOL.lu a.s.b.l.**
22, rue de Manternach
L-6681 Mertert, Luxembourg
E-mail: comite@afol.lu

## 2. What data we process

In connection with the membership application and membership, we process:

- **Identity data:** first and last name, date of birth
- **Contact data:** e-mail address, phone number, postal address
- **Forum data (if applicable):** username / WoltLab account link
- **Preferences:** preferred newsletter language (only with consent)
- **Payment data:** payment status, membership year, payment method (bank transfer/SumUp). We do not process card/account details ourselves – this is handled by the respective payment provider (see point 5).
- **Proof of consent:** the time you accepted this privacy policy and, where applicable, the newsletter.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Processing the membership application, establishing and managing membership | Art. 6(1)(b) (contract/membership) or (a) (consent) |
| Sending the newsletter | Art. 6(1)(a) (consent), revocable at any time |
| Fee administration, accounting, statutory retention | Art. 6(1)(c) (legal obligation) |
| Communication with members (e.g. confirmation e-mail) | Art. 6(1)(b) / (f) (legitimate interest) |

## 4. Recipients of the data

- **Committee / treasurer** of the association (for member and fee administration)
- **Hosting/forum:** Data is stored on the server of our WoltLab forum (afol55.afol.lu) – currently with LuxHosting (luxhosting.com), Luxembourg; after the planned migration with KittMedia, Germany. Both locations are within the EU.
- **Payment providers:** bank (transfer) or SumUp (card payment)
- **No** sharing with third parties for advertising purposes.

## 5. Payment via SumUp

If you pay the fee via the SumUp payment link, your payment data is processed directly by SumUp Limited / SumUp Payments. Their privacy policy applies: https://www.sumup.com/en-lu/privacy/ We only receive the information that a payment was made and its amount.

## 6. Retention period

- Data of **active members** is stored for the duration of membership.
- After membership ends, data is deleted once it is no longer needed – subject to statutory retention periods (e.g. accounting).
- **Withdrawn or rejected applications** are deleted or anonymised promptly.
- The **newsletter consent** remains valid until revoked.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the data stored about you (Art. 15)
- **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
- **erasure** (Art. 17) and **restriction** of processing (Art. 18)
- **data portability** (Art. 20)
- **object** to the processing (Art. 21)
- **withdraw** a given consent with effect for the future (e.g. unsubscribe from the newsletter) – Art. 7(3)

To exercise these rights, simply send an e-mail to **comite@afol.lu**.

## 8. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Luxembourg the competent authority is the **Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD)**:

Commission nationale pour la protection des données
15, Boulevard du Jazz, L-4370 Belvaux, Luxembourg
https://cnpd.public.lu

## 9. Obligation to provide data

Providing your first name, last name and e-mail address is necessary to process the membership application. Without this data we cannot process the application. The other details are optional.

## 10. Version

This privacy policy is dated **June 2026**. We reserve the right to amend it where necessary.
